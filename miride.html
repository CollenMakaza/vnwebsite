MiRide

Zimbabwe's ride, delivery & driver-earning platform

MiRide is a ride-hailing and delivery platform designed and built by Vulture Nest for Zimbabwe — Your Ride, Your Way. One app books a ride, sends a package across town, and lets drivers earn — built for our roads, our money, our languages, and our safety.

ONE APP, THREE WAYS TO MOVE

Everything Zimbabwe needs to get moving

Ride Booking

See your fare before you book — no hidden fees. Choose Economy, Comfort or Premium, pay from the MiRide Wallet, and request a female driver if you'd rather.

Package Delivery

Send a package across town by walking courier, motorcycle or car — priced and timed up front, with tracking and handover verification.

Driver Earnings

Sign up to drive or courier in under two minutes. Every driver is 100% KYC-verified — National ID, licence and vehicle registration checked.

HOW MIRIDE WORKS

From open app to arrival, in four steps

1
Set pickup, drop-off and ride tier

Confirm the route on the map and choose Economy, Comfort or Premium — the fare is shown before you book.

2
Top up and pay your way

Top up the MiRide Wallet with EcoCash, InnBucks, Visa, Mastercard or ZimSwitch — or pay cash.

3
Ride, tracked and protected

Trips share automatically with emergency contacts, with a PIN at night and SOS on standby the whole way.

4
Or send a package instead

Switch to Delivery and pick Walking Courier, Motorcycle or Car — each priced and timed up front.

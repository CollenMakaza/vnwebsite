Sparkle Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 Last updated September 2, 2026

In short

  • We collect what's needed to book and deliver a car wash: your contact details, vehicle details, service address, and payment method.
  • We only use location, camera, and push notifications for features you actively use — finding nearby washes, before/after photos, and booking updates.
  • We never sell your personal data, and we don't store your full card number.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VultureNest ("VultureNest," "Sparkle," "we," "us," or "our"), the operator of the Sparkle car wash booking application (the "App") and any related website, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

02Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what we collect automatically, and what we receive from third parties.

Information you provide

  • Account details — full name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Vehicle details — make, model, colour, and registration/number plate.
  • Booking details — service address, preferred date and time, and any special instructions.
  • Payment details — billing information needed to process a transaction (see Section 5).
  • Communications — messages you send to our support team, ratings, and reviews.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information — device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the App, pages viewed, and features used, collected via app analytics and log files.
  • Location data — with your permission (see Section 4).

Information from third parties

We may receive information from payment processors, mobile money providers, and, where you choose to sign in with a third-party account, from that provider (such as your name and email address).

03How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Sparkle account;
  • Process, confirm, and fulfil car wash bookings, including matching you with a service provider;
  • Process payments and prevent fraudulent transactions;
  • Send booking confirmations, service updates, receipts, and respond to support requests;
  • Send promotional communications, where you have not opted out;
  • Improve, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, and understand how it is used; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

04Location, camera & notifications

The App requests a small number of device permissions. Each is optional unless noted, and can be managed at any time from your device settings.

PermissionWhy we ask
Precise location To show nearby service availability, pre-fill your booking address, and help a service provider find you.
Camera & photo library To let you attach before/after photos of your vehicle to a booking, or upload a profile photo. Photos are only accessed when you choose to add one.
Push notifications To send booking confirmations, provider-arrival alerts, and status updates. Promotional notifications can be turned off separately in the App.

05Payment information

Payments made through the App are processed by third-party, PCI-DSS-compliant payment processors and mobile money providers. Sparkle does not store your full card number, card verification code, or mobile money PIN — these are handled directly by our payment partners. We retain only a record of the transaction (such as amount, date, and a masked payment reference) needed for receipts, refunds, and accounting.

06Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers — the name, vehicle details, and location needed for a wash technician or partner to fulfil your booking.
  • With payment and mobile money partners — to process transactions.
  • With service vendors — cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tools that process data on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — where required to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights and safety of Sparkle, our users, or the public, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

07Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active and as necessary to provide the Service, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

08Data security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review of our practices — designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law, including Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Object to, or request that we restrict, certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (such as location or notifications) at any time; and
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

10Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11International transfers

Some of our service providers (such as cloud hosting or analytics vendors) may process data outside Zimbabwe. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information receives an equivalent standard of protection.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice through the App or by email. Continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

02The Sparkle service

Sparkle is an online booking platform that allows users to schedule car wash and vehicle detailing services at a location of their choosing, or at a partner facility, and to pay for those services through the App. Sparkle is a technology platform: it facilitates the connection between users and independent car wash service providers but does not itself perform car wash services.

03Eligibility & accounts

  • You must provide accurate, current, and complete information when registering, and keep it up to date.
  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activity under your account.
  • Notify us immediately at info@vulturenest.org if you suspect unauthorised use of your account.

04Bookings & scheduling

When you submit a booking through the App, you are making an offer to purchase the selected service at the price shown. A booking is confirmed once you receive confirmation within the App. We will make reasonable efforts to fulfil confirmed bookings at the scheduled time, but availability of service providers may occasionally require us to propose an alternative time or location.

05Pricing & payment

Prices for available services are displayed in the App prior to confirming a booking. Prices may vary by service type, vehicle size, and location, and may change from time to time. Payment is processed through the payment method you select at checkout, including supported mobile money services and cards. All fees are inclusive of any taxes required to be disclosed at the point of sale, unless stated otherwise.

06Cancellations & refunds

  • You may cancel a booking free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled service time.
  • Cancellations made after this window, or after a service provider has been dispatched, may incur a cancellation fee of up to the full service price, to cover the provider's time.
  • If a service provider fails to arrive or a booking cannot be fulfilled due to Sparkle's or the provider's fault, you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method and may take a number of business days to reflect, depending on your payment provider.

07Service providers

Car wash services booked through Sparkle are performed by independent third-party service providers or partner facilities, not by VultureNest employees. While we take reasonable steps to vet providers on our platform, providers are solely responsible for the manner in which they perform the service. Sparkle is not a party to, and assumes no liability for, the contract for services formed between you and the provider, beyond facilitating the booking and payment.

08Vehicle condition & liability

We recommend taking photos of your vehicle before the service starts. Any claim relating to loss of or damage to your vehicle during a service must be reported to us at info@vulturenest.org within 24 hours of the service, with supporting photos where possible, so that we can investigate with the service provider.

A garage, run from three connected apps

MotaMate is a garage management platform designed and built by Vulture Nest. It gives owners a dashboard to run the business, gives front-desk staff and technicians a phone-based tool for the shop floor, and gives car owners an app to book, track and approve their own service — all three talking to the same job, in real time.

HOW MOTAMATE WORKS

From booking to paid, in six steps

1
Vulture Nest onboards the garage

The garage's account, first branch and owner login are created and handed over — no self-signup, so every garage starts from a clean, correct setup.

2
The owner sets up the garage

Branches, staff and roles, and parts pricing are added on the web dashboard, so the team is ready to take work.

Web Dashboard
3
The customer books a service

Choose the vehicle, branch, service type and time slot from their phone — the request lands straight on the garage's Bookings screen.

Customer App
4
Front desk checks the car in

Checking in turns the booking into a job card, then the advisor walks the car with a phone, ticking off a photo-backed pre-repair checklist.

Staff App
5
The work is quoted, approved and assigned

The advisor prices parts and labour and sends a quote; the customer approves it in-app; a technician is assigned and gets to work on their own job list.

Web + Customer + Staff App
6
The job is finished, invoiced and paid

The job moves through quality check to ready-for-invoice, the cashier takes payment, and the customer watches the whole timeline from their phone.

THE CAST

One role, one clear job

When an owner adds a staff member, they pick a single role — it decides exactly what that person can see and do, on the dashboard and on the staff app.

Owner

Everything, including Settings, reports and approvals.

Manager

Day-to-day operations, most screens, approvals.

Service Advisor

Customers, vehicles, check-ins, job cards, checklists, quotes.

Foreman

Assigns work, moves jobs through stages, quality sign-off.

Technician

Their assigned jobs and checklists, on the staff app.

Cashier

Opens/closes the till, raises invoices, takes payments.

Storekeeper

Inventory: parts and prices, stock, purchase orders.

Scoped by design

Every garage only ever sees its own data — never another garage's.
